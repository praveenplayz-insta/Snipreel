Brain engine

Intro voice

Smart basic works instantly with no download and no key. It writes titles and hashtags from the transcript and builds the intro from rule-like sentences it finds anywhere in the video. On-device AI writes more natural copy with no key, but needs a one-time download of about 0.5 GB and is meant for desktop. Gemini (free key from aistudio.google.com) writes the best copy and sends only transcript text to Google, never your video; Google may use free-tier requests to improve its products. The key stays in this browser. On phones the intro voice starts switched off because the voice model is heavy; switch it on to try it.

Sound effects made in your browser, free and royalty free

Tap to hear them

The AI reads the transcript and adds a whoosh at transitions and long pauses, a cash sound on money and numbers, a boom on shock words, cheers on wins, a sad tune on fails and a soft ding on questions. Subtle uses fewer and skips the small ones. Effects are simple synthesised sounds, not studio recordings.

    4. FinishColor grade and word-by-word captions are baked into each clip, ready to post.
